The Validation Layer for Africa's Web3 Market

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to what we hear most often. Can't find yours? Send us a message.

What is Sofe Node? ▼
Sofe Node is Africa's Web3 distribution infrastructure — connecting global protocols to African users and talent through campus activations, bootcamps, and our community network.
How do I join a bootcamp? ▼
Click "Get Started Today" on the homepage to register for the next cohort. Bootcamps run regularly across physical and virtual formats, with tuition subsidized by our global ecosystem partners.
How does the affiliate program work? ▼
Once registered, you get a unique referral link. Earn 10% at Level 1 (Newbie) and 15% at Level 2 (Ambassador) on every successful signup. Commissions are paid directly to your Nigerian bank account via Paystack.
How can my brand partner with Sofe Node? ▼
We offer market distribution, talent sponsorship, campus activation, and conference sponsorship packages. Click "Scale in Africa" or reach out via this form and our partnerships team will respond within 48 hours.
What is the BCE? ▼
The Blockchain Career Expo (BCE) is Sofe Node's annual flagship event — bringing together Web3 professionals, protocols, and talent for keynotes, a career fair, and an exhibition floor across our node networks.
