Quick Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I join a bootcamp?
Click "Get Started Today" on the homepage or Academy page to register for the next cohort. Bootcamps run regularly in virtual and physical formats, with fees heavily subsidised by our global partners.
How can my brand partner with Sofe Node?
We offer market distribution, talent sponsorship, campus activation, and conference sponsorship. Fill the contact form above selecting "Brand / Protocol" and our partnerships team will respond within 48 hours.
How does the affiliate program work?
Once registered, you get a unique referral link. Earn 10% at Level 1 (Newbie) and 15% at Level 2 (Ambassador) on every successful signup. Commissions are paid directly to your Nigerian bank account via Paystack.
Is there a fee to join Sofe Node?
Creating a free Sofian account costs nothing. Course fees are heavily subsidised (up to 80%) by our global partners. The remaining fee (around ₦2,000 for certification) covers admin and credentialing costs.
Can I open a campus chapter at my university?
Yes. We actively expand our university node network. Reach out via the form above selecting "University / Campus representative" and our expansion team will be in touch.
What is the BCE event?
The Blockchain Career Expo (BCE) is Sofe Node's annual flagship conference — bringing together Web3 professionals, protocols, and talent for keynotes, a career fair, and exhibitions across our node networks.
